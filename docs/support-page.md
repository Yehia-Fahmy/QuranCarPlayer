# Support — Quran Carplayer

## Contact

Email [yehia.fahmy@uwaterloo.ca](mailto:yehia.fahmy@uwaterloo.ca) for help. Include your device model, OS version, app version from **Settings → About**, and what happened. For playback issues, include the reciter, surah, and whether you were streaming, listening offline, or using CarPlay.

## Listening and downloads

Choose a reciter, then tap a surah to start listening. The app saves your place on the device. Open **Settings → Manage Downloads** to play, retry, or remove a saved recording.

Build 7 streams from MP3Quran. Tap **Download for Offline** in the player to save that recording until you remove it. Build 6 uses Quran Foundation recordings with a seven-day offline period. After upgrading, previously saved Foundation recordings can still be played from Downloads until their original expiry. Downloaded audio is excluded from device backups.

If a stream stops, check your connection and tap **Retry**. For offline playback, open the saved recording from Downloads. To clear all local app data, delete the app from the device.

## Themes and screen sizes

In builds with **Settings → Theme**, choose Folio, Studio, Nocturne, Grove, or Copper. **System Appearance** follows your device and uses Folio in light mode and Nocturne in dark mode. Your selection is saved on that device.

On supported iPad and iPhone Duo builds, wider windows show the surah list beside the player. Narrow the window or close the Duo to use one pane at a time. In the two-pane layout, open the library options menu (…) to reach Settings.

## Sources and language

Open **Settings → Privacy & Support → Sources** to find the recording providers, reciter credits, portrait sources, and license links. Earlier versions show credits directly in Settings. Most portraits are bundled. Minshawi’s historical portrait can be fetched from Wikimedia Commons in the Canadian and U.S. App Store storefronts.

In **Settings → Language**, choose System, English, or Arabic. Arabic uses right-to-left navigation. Attribution text can appear in its original language.

## CarPlay

Connect your iPhone to a CarPlay-compatible vehicle and open Quran Carplayer on the car display. Use **Current Surah** to find the active chapter in a long list. If audio does not resume, check the automatic-resume setting and tap Play. A deliberate pause or another app already playing can prevent automatic playback. Adjust phone settings while parked.

## Privacy and pricing

Quran Carplayer is free and has no subscriptions, ads, or accounts. Preferences and playback progress stay on your device and may be included in Apple device backups. Streaming and downloads contact the audio provider. Read the [Privacy Policy](https://yehia-fahmy.github.io/QuranCarPlayer/privacy/) for details.
