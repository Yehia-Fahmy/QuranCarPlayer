### Build 7 restores the original recitations

The iPhone and CarPlay catalog uses the original nine MP3Quran collections for fresh chapter selections and downloads. Existing MP3Quran downloads retain their identity and have no scheduled expiry. Eligible build 6 Quran Foundation files can be played explicitly from Downloads or resumed at their original position until their original seven days expire. The live MP3Quran API matched every selected edition, and all 1,026 chapter URLs returned partial MP3 content. [Catalog verification](app-store-connect/rights-evidence/mp3quran-2026-11-27.md) · [Playback and storage behavior](OFFLINE_DOWNLOADS.md)

### Build 6 audio provider

The iPhone fetches new streams and downloads directly from Quran Foundation's QuranicAudio host. All 1,026 generated URLs matched the checked chapter manifests. A live download of Maher's Al-Fatihah through the production transfer client saved 753,249 bytes and decoded as 62.59 seconds of audio. Each new private download lasts seven days. Build 5 MP3Quran downloads keep their collection identity and original retention. A saved position carries over only for the same performance. [Recording inventory and sources](app-store-connect/rights-evidence/quran-foundation-catalog.md) · [Offline behavior](OFFLINE_DOWNLOADS.md)

## Decisions and acceptance before submission

- **Build delivery.** On November 27, 2026 at 5:47 p.m. Toronto time, Xcode uploaded iOS 1.0.0 (6) to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/apps/6835290983).
