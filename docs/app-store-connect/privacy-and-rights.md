# Build 6 privacy and content-rights answers

Prepared November 27, 2026 for Quran Carplayer 1.0.0 (6). This worksheet records proposed App Store Connect entries and their evidence. The signed app has no account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer backend. Its two built-in network features deliver audio and one regional portrait. [Production catalog](../../Packages/QuranCarPlayerCore/Sources/QuranCarPlayerCore/Catalog/QuranFoundationCatalog.swift) · [Portrait downloader](../../App/Sources/Artwork/RegionalMinshawiPortraitStore.swift) · [Published policy](https://yehia-fahmy.github.io/QuranCarPlayer/privacy/)

## App Privacy

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Proposed answer **Yes**, counting the external services integrated for direct media delivery. Wikimedia documents retaining media requests beyond the time needed to serve them. Apple asks for the union of data practices across regions, even where only some people use the feature. The privacy policy URL is `https://yehia-fahmy.github.io/QuranCarPlayer/privacy/`. [Apple’s definitions and IP guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) · [Wikimedia request pipeline](https://wikitech.wikimedia.org/wiki/Data_Platform/Data_Lake/Traffic/Webrequest)

**Data used to track the user** — Proposed answer **No**. The production app sends no account or advertising identifier. The providers’ published policies describe service operation and measurement. The evidence reviewed does not indicate cross-company targeted advertising, advertising measurement or sharing with a data broker for the direct media requests. [Build manifest](../../App/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) · [Quran Foundation policy](https://quran.com/privacy) · [Wikimedia privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy)

**Linked to the user** — Select **Yes** for the entries below where raw IP is retained alongside the event. This is a conservative reading of Apple’s rule that de-identification must precede retention. A later aggregate or purge does not change how the original record was stored. Wikimedia’s identifying logs are generally purged or de-identified within 90 days, subject to documented exceptions. [Apple’s linking criteria](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) · [Wikimedia retention guidelines](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Legal:Wikimedia_Foundation_Data_Retention_Guidelines)

### Evidence for data types and purposes

- **Coarse Location** — **Analytics** and **App Functionality**. Wikimedia enriches request records with country, subdivision and, where available, city inferred from IP. Its policies describe service measurement and security. The app uses StoreKit storefront eligibility locally and requests no device location permission. [Wikimedia request fields](https://wikitech.wikimedia.org/wiki/Data_Platform/Data_Lake/Traffic/Webrequest#Current_Schema) · [Wikimedia privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy)
- **Product Interaction** — **Analytics** and **App Functionality** for Wikimedia media request counts and delivery. Its fixed portrait URL can indicate that artwork was requested. The app may request this image while building a list, so that request alone cannot establish which recording played. If Quran Foundation retains MP3 request paths, the same category covers the selected reciter and chapter. Confirm the audio host’s purposes in the provider question below. [Wikimedia media-request statistics](https://doc.wikimedia.org/generated-data-platform/aqs/analytics-api/reference/media-files.html) · [Apple’s usage-data examples](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)
- **Performance Data** and **Other Diagnostic Data** — **App Functionality** for delivery timing, HTTP status, and technical client information recorded for media delivery and troubleshooting. Wikimedia retains these fields beside the source IP. Its servers measure response time. The app has no crash-reporting endpoint. [Wikimedia log fields](https://wikitech.wikimedia.org/wiki/Data_Platform/Data_Lake/Traffic/Webrequest#Current_Schema)

These proposed categories describe the known Wikimedia path. The audio provider can change the final purposes or add a category. IP addresses have no single mandatory label under Apple’s guidance. Add **Device ID** if a provider uses IP or another value to identify a device. Content requests can suggest interest in religion. The app does not request a religious affiliation or construct a belief profile. Declare **Sensitive Info** if a provider actually derives or retains that information. [Apple’s data types and examples](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)

**Local and optional activity.** Preferences, download inventory, playback position and files remain on the phone. Apple controls system backup and Now Playing presentation. The app opens external credit and support links in the system handler. Users initiate any support email in their mail client. There is no in-app collection of email, uploaded audio, contacts, browsing history or account credentials. Earlier MP3Quran recordings are read from local storage. A first-time installation has none. [Policy and deletion options](https://yehia-fahmy.github.io/QuranCarPlayer/privacy/) · [Source selection](../../App/Sources/AppEnvironment.swift)

**Open provider question.** Quran Foundation’s website policy discusses server logs, and its email section specifies a retention period for newsletter engagement. It gives no retention period or purposes specific to unauthenticated requests for `download.quranicaudio.com` MP3s. The policy on QuranicAudio’s site for its own iOS app covers a different product. Apple’s label needs the data categories, purposes, linkage and tracking answers. An exact day count is useful for the public policy, but the threshold for the label is whether readable data is retained beyond real-time delivery. [Foundation policy and privacy contact](https://quran.com/privacy) · [QuranicAudio iOS policy](https://quranicaudio.com/privacy.html) · [Apple’s real-time delivery exception](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)

Question for Quran Foundation’s published privacy contact, `info@quran.foundation`:

> Quran Carplayer is a free iPhone app that streams and downloads MP3s directly from download.quranicaudio.com without accounts or API tokens. For these direct requests, what do the Foundation and its CDN retain after delivery, and for how long? Is the IP address or another identifier kept alongside the reciter/chapter URL, timestamp or client information? Please identify any location derivation, device or listener identification, religious-interest profiling, service analytics, advertising, or sharing, including the purposes and whether records are anonymized before storage. This will help us describe your practices accurately on Apple’s App Privacy label.

## Content Rights

**Does the app contain, show or access third-party content?** **Yes.** The applicable choice is the one covering apps authorized to show or access third-party content. Keep the following basis with the app record. Apple asks for permission in every offered region and can request evidence of the audio download authorization. [App Store Connect content rights](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/) · [App Review Guidelines §§5.2.2–5.2.3](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#intellectual-property)

- **New recordings.** Quran Foundation explicitly covers direct streaming and downloading from this host. Build 6 credits the Foundation and reciter and keeps user-requested copies in private app storage for seven days. Users can delete them. The release contains no provider MP3s and offers no file export or rehosting. Nine editions cover 114 chapters each. The published direct-audio terms have no stated regional carve-out and grant conditional, revocable use. The app uses the seven-day option without Content Sync. [Provider authorization, §3.1](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/#31-acceptable-use) · [Provider FAQ](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/docs/tutorials/faq/#audio-and-recitations) · [Verified editions](rights-evidence/quran-foundation-catalog.md)
- **Previously downloaded recordings.** An upgrade preserves a listener’s existing local MP3Quran files. New streams and downloads use Quran Foundation. MP3Quran’s public copying notice is retained as historical evidence. The previous provider’s CDN practices do not describe this build’s new network traffic. [Legacy provider evidence](rights-evidence/mp3quran-2026-11-27.md)
- **Portraits.** Eight photos have recorded reuse terms and in-app credits. Mishary’s image uses a noncommercial grant on the basis of the publisher’s free, ad-free nonprofit use. Three CC BY-SA adaptations carry share-alike notices. Minshawi’s 1954 photo is delivered only for confirmed Canadian and U.S. storefronts, backed by the documented copyright basis in each. All other storefronts use the app’s own geometric artwork for that reciter. Use worldwide-cleared imagery in shared store screenshots. Recheck the noncommercial license if monetization changes.
