# Build 7 privacy and content-rights answers

Updated November 28, 2026 for Quran Carplayer 1.0.0 (7). This worksheet records proposed App Store Connect entries and their evidence. The app has no account, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or developer backend. Its two built-in network features deliver audio and one regional portrait. [Production catalog](../../Packages/QuranCarPlayerCore/Sources/QuranCarPlayerCore/Catalog/MP3QuranCatalog.swift) · [Portrait downloader](../../App/Sources/Artwork/RegionalMinshawiPortraitStore.swift) · [Published policy](https://yehia-fahmy.github.io/QuranCarPlayer/privacy/)

## App Privacy

**Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** Proposed answer **Yes**, counting the external services integrated for direct media delivery. Wikimedia documents retaining media requests beyond the time needed to serve them. Apple asks for the union of data practices across regions, even where only some people use the feature. The privacy policy URL is `https://yehia-fahmy.github.io/QuranCarPlayer/privacy/`. [Apple’s definitions and IP guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) · [Wikimedia request pipeline](https://wikitech.wikimedia.org/wiki/Data_Platform/Data_Lake/Traffic/Webrequest)

**Data used to track the user** — Proposed answer **No**. The production app sends no account or advertising identifier. The Wikimedia policy describes service operation and measurement. MP3Quran confirmed that it uses CDN logs for security and delivery, does not use them to track users and does not share them with third parties. [November 28 provider reply](rights-evidence/mp3quran-2026-11-27.md) [Build manifest](../../App/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) · [MP3Quran policy](https://www.mp3quran.net/privacy-en.html) · [Wikimedia privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy)

**Linked to the user** — The draft treats Wikimedia records retained with raw IP addresses as potentially linked. Review each declared data type using Apple’s linkage criteria. The MP3Quran media host’s treatment of identifiable requests is still unknown. Wikimedia’s identifying logs are generally purged or de-identified within 90 days, subject to documented exceptions. [Apple’s linking criteria](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) · [Wikimedia retention guidelines](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Legal:Wikimedia_Foundation_Data_Retention_Guidelines)

### Evidence for data types and purposes

- **Coarse Location** — **Analytics** and **App Functionality**. Wikimedia enriches request records with country, subdivision and, where available, city inferred from IP. Its policies describe service measurement and security. The app uses StoreKit storefront eligibility locally and requests no device location permission. [Wikimedia request fields](https://wikitech.wikimedia.org/wiki/Data_Platform/Data_Lake/Traffic/Webrequest#Current_Schema) · [Wikimedia privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy)
- **Product Interaction** — **Analytics** and **App Functionality** for Wikimedia media request counts and delivery. Its fixed portrait URL can indicate that artwork was requested. The app may request this image while building a list, so that request alone cannot establish which recording played. If MP3Quran retains MP3 request paths, the same category covers the selected reciter and chapter. MP3Quran confirmed security and delivery uses. The fields it retains remain under review. [Wikimedia media-request statistics](https://doc.wikimedia.org/generated-data-platform/aqs/analytics-api/reference/media-files.html) · [Apple’s usage-data examples](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)
- **Performance Data** and **Other Diagnostic Data** — **App Functionality** for delivery timing, HTTP status, and technical client information recorded for media delivery and troubleshooting. Wikimedia retains these fields beside the source IP. Its servers measure response time. The app has no crash-reporting endpoint. [Wikimedia log fields](https://wikitech.wikimedia.org/wiki/Data_Platform/Data_Lake/Traffic/Webrequest#Current_Schema)

These proposed categories describe the known Wikimedia path. The audio provider can change the final purposes or add a category. IP addresses have no single mandatory label under Apple’s guidance. Add **Device ID** if a provider uses IP or another value to identify a device. Content requests can suggest interest in religion. The app does not request a religious affiliation or construct a belief profile. Declare **Sensitive Info** if a provider actually derives or retains that information. [Apple’s data types and examples](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)

**Local and optional activity.** Preferences, download inventory, playback position and files remain on the phone. Apple controls system backup and Now Playing presentation. The app opens external credit and support links in the system handler. Users initiate any support email in their mail client. There is no in-app collection of email, uploaded audio, contacts, browsing history or account credentials. Quran Foundation recordings saved under build 6 are played from private device storage until their original expiry. A first-time installation has none. [Policy and deletion options](https://yehia-fahmy.github.io/QuranCarPlayer/privacy/) · [Source selection](../../App/Sources/AppEnvironment.swift)

**Open CDN question.** MP3Quran says it retains general access logs as needed for operational security. The November 28 follow-up asks whether the CDN stores the IP address alongside the requested MP3 path after delivery, whether it anonymizes the address before logging, and how long it normally keeps that record. [MP3Quran’s policy](https://www.mp3quran.net/privacy-en.html) · [Provider correspondence record](rights-evidence/mp3quran-2026-11-27.md) · [Apple’s real-time delivery exception](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/)

The separate inquiry to Quran Foundation concerned build 6. Build 7 makes its new audio requests to MP3Quran. The Foundation’s response applies to the earlier build.

## Content Rights

**Does the app contain, show or access third-party content?** **Yes.** Apple can request permission covering streaming and user-initiated downloads in all offered regions. The November 28 provider reply documents worldwide authorization for the restored MP3Quran recordings. [App Store Connect content rights](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/) · [App Review Guidelines §§5.2.2–5.2.3](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#intellectual-property)

- **New recordings.** MP3Quran’s published copyright notice permits developers to use site links and copy material. Build 7 streams from the provider and allows a user to save individual recordings in private storage until removal. The nine original editions each cover 114 chapters. MP3Quran expressly authorized all nine editions for streaming and offline listening in Quran Carplayer worldwide. No specific attribution is required. [MP3Quran copyright notice](https://www.mp3quran.net/privacy-en.html) · [Selected collections and written permission](rights-evidence/mp3quran-2026-11-27.md)
- **Existing recordings.** Downloads from earlier MP3Quran builds remain playable. The app also keeps eligible Foundation downloads inside the app for the remainder of their original seven days. [Build 6 offline terms and editions](rights-evidence/quran-foundation-catalog.md)

- **Portraits.** Eight photos have recorded reuse terms and in-app credits. Mishary’s image uses a noncommercial grant on the basis of the publisher’s free, ad-free nonprofit use. Three CC BY-SA adaptations carry share-alike notices. Minshawi’s 1954 photo is delivered only for confirmed Canadian and U.S. storefronts, backed by the documented copyright basis in each. All other storefronts use the app’s own geometric artwork for that reciter. Use worldwide-cleared imagery in shared store screenshots. Recheck the noncommercial license if monetization changes.
