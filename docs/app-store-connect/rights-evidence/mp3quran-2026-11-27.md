# MP3Quran recording permissions

This record covers the original MP3Quran editions used through build 5 and restored in build 7. Build 6 used [Quran Foundation editions](quran-foundation-catalog.md).

Verified November 27, 2026 for Quran Carplayer’s nine Hafs an Asim collections. The iPhone app streams individual recordings directly from the provider. A listener can also save individual recordings for later playback inside the app.

## Provider statements

The English contact page permits developers to copy material or use site URLs and directs developers to give Apple the provider's privacy page as evidence. [Contact notice](https://www.mp3quran.net/eng/contact-us) · [Page designated for Apple](https://mp3quran.net/eng/privacy)

The English privacy page's Copyrights section grants permission to “copy any material or use any link on the website.” [Copyright notice](https://www.mp3quran.net/privacy-en.html)

## Collections in this build

The catalog identifies Maher Al-Muaiqly (moshaf 102), Mishary Rashid Alafasy (123), Abdul Rahman Al-Sudais (54), Saud Al-Shuraim (31), Saad Al-Ghamdi (30), Mahmoud Khalil Al-Hussary (118), Mohammed Siddiq Al-Minshawi (112), Abdulbasit Abdulsamad (53), and Yasser Al-Dosari (92). Each includes chapters 1 through 114. The snapshot records the exact server and reciter identifier separately for each collection. [Bundled catalog](../../../Packages/QuranCarPlayerCore/Sources/QuranCarPlayerCore/Resources/mp3quran-catalog.json)

## Verification on November 27, 2026

The production API matched all nine bundled reciter IDs, moshaf IDs, base URLs and complete chapter lists. Requests for the first 64 bytes of each of the 1,026 MP3 URLs returned HTTP 206 and `audio/mpeg`. Eight editions began with an ID3 or MPEG header. The 114 Abdulbasit files use leading padding, and each contained a frame sync within the first 1,024 bytes. Apple’s audio tools recognized the complete Abdulbasit Al-Fatihah as a 47.02-second stereo MP3. The range checks establish availability and file format at the time checked; they do not establish full-length quality or rights. [Production reciter API](https://mp3quran.net/api/v3/reciters?language=eng)

## Submission decision

Quran Carplayer is free and has no ads or purchases. The app streams the provider’s original files and lets a listener keep an unmodified copy privately inside the app. The public copying notice gives no territorial limit. It leaves open the scope of the underlying recording permissions and persistent offline storage in all selected storefronts. The provider’s website policy lists server logging of visitor IP addresses, visit times, country and referring URLs. It does not describe retention of the listener’s IP alongside a chapter request to `cdn.mp3quran.net`. The earlier request for worldwide clarification covers these points. [Copyright and privacy notice](https://www.mp3quran.net/privacy-en.html)

Build 7 includes eight licensed portraits and a Canadian/U.S. storefront option for Minshawi’s historical photograph. [Artwork source and license records](../../ARTWORK_ATTRIBUTION.md)

## Alternatives considered before provider outreach

- **An explicitly reusable recording.** The Commons contributor Ibrahimmusa4's Al-Fatihah file is identified as the creator's own recording and released under CC0. It supplies a clear route to bundling or hosting that particular performance. It covers one chapter and a different voice. [Original recording and dedication](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AlF%C4%81tihatulKit%C4%81b.ogg)
- **A service with explicit offline terms.** Quran Foundation allows streaming from its audio hosts and user-requested downloads held privately on the device. The app must credit the Foundation and reciter, implement deletion, and for recordings in Content Sync, synchronize at least weekly when reachable and apply removals and URL changes. The terms prohibit rehosting. Build 6 used this provider with seven-day private copies. The [chapter catalog comparison](quran-foundation-catalog.md) records coverage for the nine requested reciters. [Developer FAQ and terms](https://api-docs.quran.foundation/docs/tutorials/faq/#can-my-app-let-users-download-recitations-for-offline-listening)
- **A service with published log limits.** Al Quran Cloud permits streaming, embedding and downloading for personal and educational use and states a 29-day maximum log retention. Its recordings remain subject to the reciters' rights. Its policy cannot answer retention questions for MP3Quran. [Provider terms](https://alquran.cloud/terms-and-conditions)

A free, nonprofit release can satisfy a noncommercial restriction where one applies. Permission to distribute the underlying sound recordings still depends on the applicable grant. Build 7 restores all nine collections.
