# MP3Quran recording permissions

Verified November 27, 2026 for QuranCarPlayer's nine Hafs an Asim collections. The iPhone app streams individual recordings directly from the provider. A listener can also save individual recordings for later playback inside the app.

## Provider statements

The English contact page permits developers to copy material or use site URLs and directs developers to give Apple the provider's privacy page as evidence. [Contact notice](https://www.mp3quran.net/eng/contact-us) · [Page designated for Apple](https://mp3quran.net/eng/privacy)

The English privacy page's Copyrights section grants permission to “copy any material or use any link on the website.” [Copyright notice](https://www.mp3quran.net/privacy-en.html)

## Collections in this build

The catalog identifies Maher Al-Muaiqly (moshaf 102), Mishary Rashid Alafasy (123), Abdul Rahman Al-Sudais (54), Saud Al-Shuraim (31), Saad Al-Ghamdi (30), Mahmoud Khalil Al-Hussary (118), Mohammed Siddiq Al-Minshawi (112), Abdulbasit Abdulsamad (53), and Yasser Al-Dosari (92). Each includes chapters 1 through 114. The snapshot records the exact server and reciter identifier separately for each collection.
