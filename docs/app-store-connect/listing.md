**Subtitle**

**Promotional text**

Choose from nine reciters before you drive. Save a surah on your iPhone for offline listening, then open it from your CarPlay display.

**Description**

Listen to all 114 surahs of the Quran on iPhone or iPad, or in the car with Apple CarPlay. Choose among nine reciters, including Maher Al-Muaiqly, Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and Mahmoud Khalil Al-Hussary. Each recites the complete Quran in Arabic.

Before a trip, open a surah and tap Download for Offline. That recording will play even when your route takes you out of coverage. Use Downloads to play or remove saved recordings at any time.

In the car, browse by surah or reciter on the CarPlay display. When you return, Continue Listening takes you back to your saved place. You can also control playback from your iPhone’s Lock Screen.

On iPad, browse the chapter list alongside the player in a wide window. In Settings, choose English or Arabic and preview listening themes before applying one. Quran Carplayer is free and has no ads, subscriptions, or account registration.

**Notes for Review**

Quran Carplayer 1.0.0 (8) is a free iPhone and iPad audio app with CarPlay Audio on iPhone. The app opens directly to the surah list.

1. In the compact layout, tap Reciters at the upper left. In the wide layout, open the library options menu (…) and tap Reciters. Select Maher Al-Muaiqly and tap Done. Select Al-Fatihah (chapter 1) to start playback. The player shows the reciter and chapter artwork. Pause partway through to try Continue Listening.
2. While online, open Al-Fatihah and tap Download for Offline. Wait for Available Offline. To verify a fresh offline start, close the app, turn off cellular data and Wi-Fi, reopen the app, and select Al-Fatihah with the same reciter. Use Downloads on the Surahs screen to see or remove the saved recording. MP3Quran downloads stay in the app until removed.
3. With a CarPlay display connected, open Quran Carplayer on the display. Choose Reciters, select a reciter, then choose a chapter under Surahs. Smaller vehicle lists use numbered ranges. Open Listen to return to Now Playing or resume an interrupted chapter. The same saved recording can play offline on CarPlay.
4. On iPhone, open Settings in Quran Carplayer to switch between English and Arabic or review the audio and artwork credits. Recordings stream directly from MP3Quran. Private downloads are playable only in the app. Settings links to the sources and licenses for the portraits. The historical Al-Minshawi photograph is fetched only for confirmed Canadian and U.S. storefronts. Other storefronts display geometric chapter artwork for this reciter.

On November 28, 2026, MP3Quran gave Quran Carplayer written permission for worldwide streaming and in-app offline listening for all nine included editions. The original confirmation is available on request. The public copyright notice is at https://www.mp3quran.net/privacy-en.html

Portrait sources, licenses and territorial permissions are documented at https://github.com/Yehia-Fahmy/QuranCarPlayer/blob/main/docs/ARTWORK_ATTRIBUTION.md
