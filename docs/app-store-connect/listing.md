## Product page

**Name**

Quran Carplayer

**Subtitle**

Recitations for the road

**Promotional text**

Choose from nine reciters before you drive. Download a surah on your iPhone for seven days of offline listening, then open it from your CarPlay display.

**Description**

Listen to all 114 surahs of the Quran on your iPhone or with Apple CarPlay. Choose among nine reciters, including Maher Al-Muaiqly, Mishary Rashid Alafasy, and Mahmoud Khalil Al-Hussary. Each recites the complete Quran in Arabic.

Before a trip, open a surah and tap Download for 7 Days. That recording will play even when your route takes you out of coverage. Download it again after it expires, or remove it at any time from Downloads.

In the car, browse by surah or reciter on the CarPlay display. When you return, Continue Listening takes you back to your saved place. You can also control playback from your iPhone’s Lock Screen.

Choose an English or Arabic interface in Settings. Quran Carplayer is free and has no ads, subscriptions, or account registration.

## App Review information

**Sign-in required** — No. **Contact** — Yehia Fahmy, yehia.fahmy@uwaterloo.ca. Use the review contact’s verified phone number directly in App Store Connect. Its international-format number belongs in the private review contact field.

**Notes for Review**

Quran Carplayer 1.0.0 (6) is a free iPhone audio app with the CarPlay Audio entitlement. The app opens directly to the surah list.

1. On iPhone, tap Reciters at the upper left, select Maher Al-Muaiqly, and tap Done. Select Al-Fatihah (chapter 1) to start playback. The player shows the reciter and chapter artwork. Pause partway through to try Continue Listening.
2. While online, open Al-Fatihah and tap Download for 7 Days. Wait for Available Offline. To verify a fresh offline start, close the app, turn off cellular data and Wi-Fi, reopen the app, and select Al-Fatihah with the same reciter. Use Downloads on the Surahs screen to see or remove the saved recording. New downloads are private to the app and expire after seven days.
3. With a CarPlay display connected, open Quran Carplayer on the display. Choose Reciters, select a reciter, then choose a chapter under Surahs. Smaller vehicle lists use numbered ranges. Open Listen to return to Now Playing or resume an interrupted chapter. The same saved recording can play offline on CarPlay.
4. On iPhone, open Settings in Quran Carplayer to switch between English and Arabic or review the audio and artwork credits. Recordings stream directly from Quran Foundation. Private downloads are playable only in the app. Settings links to the sources and licenses for the portraits. The historical Al-Minshawi photograph is fetched only for confirmed Canadian and U.S. storefronts. Other storefronts display geometric chapter artwork for this reciter.

Quran Foundation's direct playback and download terms are available at https://api-docs.quran.foundation/legal/developer-terms/#31-acceptable-use

Portrait sources, licenses and territorial permissions are documented at https://github.com/Yehia-Fahmy/QuranCarPlayer/blob/main/docs/ARTWORK_ATTRIBUTION.md
